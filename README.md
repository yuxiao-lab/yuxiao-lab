## Research

- **Nucleic acid detection:** Develop sensitive, high-resolution methods to profile RNA-protein interactions, including in limited or challenging biological samples.
- **Spatially resolved RNA sequencing:** Build in situ approaches to connect RNA regulation with tissue architecture and the cellular microenvironment.
- **RNA-based therapeutics:** Apply insights into RNA-binding proteins and regulatory circuits to the design of next-generation RNA therapeutics.
